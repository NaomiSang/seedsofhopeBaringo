About Us

Seeds of Hope Baringo is a community-driven initiative dedicated to supporting drought-affected families, vulnerable children and struggling households in Baringo County.

We work through partnerships, donations and collective action, because we believe every act of kindness can change a life.

Our Mission

To fight hunger, restore hope, empower families and promote sustainable community development through partnerships, donations and collective action.

Our Vision

A Baringo where no child goes to bed hungry and every family has the support to recover from drought and thrive.

Our Programs

Practical help that reaches families where they are.

Food Relief

Food packs and meals for households hit hardest by drought.

Child Support

Nutrition, school essentials and care for vulnerable children.

Family Empowerment

Helping struggling households rebuild their livelihoods.

Community Development

Partnering with locals on water, farming and long-term resilience.

Life in Baringo South

Long dry spells have left the ground bare and families struggling to put food on the table.

Why Support Us

Prolonged dry spells leave many families in Baringo without enough food or income. Your support turns into direct help for people who need it now, while building resilience for the seasons ahead.

  • Gifts go to families and children in Baringo County
  • Run by the community, so help reaches the right homes
  • Every contribution, big or small, makes a difference
